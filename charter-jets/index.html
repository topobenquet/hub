Charter Jets Inc: Paid Media Program

This is the working hub for the Google Ads program. Every document here is live, meaning it gets updated as the work progresses rather than sent once and forgotten. The short version of where things stand is below, and each document goes deeper.

Where we are today

There are two Google Ads accounts, and the picture changed substantially once we audited the second one.

Account 813-172-6321 spent $4,030 and produced 3 real quote requests. It was optimising toward a conversion that was not a lead, its geographic targeting was open to the entire world, and 44% of spend went to people who were never going to book. Detail in the account audit.

Account 645-044-1013 is the one we will use, and it is a much better built account: $67,621 of real spend, sound conversion tracking, correct geographic targeting, and only 4.6% wasted spend. Detail in account history.

Two things came out of that second audit that change the plan:

$12,133 was spent sending paid traffic to a landing page that was returning a 500 error behind an expired SSL certificate. Every ad in the account pointed at lp.charterjetsinc.com, which broke in November 2025 and was still being paid for through March 2026. Nothing in Google Ads tells you the destination died.

We now have real benchmarks instead of estimates. $7.24 per click, a 0.66% click to inquiry rate, and $1,094 per inquiry. That last number is six times worse than the industry estimate we had been planning against, and it reshapes the economics.

The three things that matter most right now

1. The whole programme reduces to one number: the capture rate. At the 0.66% of clicks the old landing page turned into an inquiry, breaking even would require closing 37% of them, which nobody does. Roughly double that capture, to 1.2%, and an ordinary 20% close rate breaks even. You have already helped by moving the Avinode commission to 15%. The rest is the landing page and counting every lead path, not bidding. Full model in unit economics.

2. Measurement has to be built before launch, not verified during month 1. Details in the measurement plan.

3. The first month's report will look worse than what you are seeing today. We will go from thousands of recorded "conversions" to a few dozen real leads, and from thousands of cheap clicks to hundreds of expensive ones. That is the goal, not a failure, and we would rather say so in writing now than explain it after the fact.

The two numbers we still need

Everything above is modelled. Two inputs would replace the modelling with your reality:

  • Lead to booked flight close rate. There are 50 recorded inquiries in the account from 2024 and 2025. If you can tell us how many became flights, we can replace the model with your actual number.
  • Average flights per client over their lifetime. Decides whether one flight per client is survivable. At the current markup, one flight per client is break even and two works.

And one access request has moved to the top: GoDaddy. lp.charterjetsinc.com already exists and already belongs to you, so pointing it at the new landing page solves the domain question outright.
