Geographic targeting, campaign by campaign

S | Charter | FL

The whole state of Florida. Not individual metro areas as the account does today, which excludes Naples, Boca Raton and the Keys, all of which are pure charter markets. No exclusions.

S | Charter | NY

The New York charter client does not depart from New York City. They depart from Teterboro, Westchester and Republic. Targeting the city alone, as the account does today, excludes the actual buyer.

Include: New York State, New Jersey and Connecticut. Bid adjustment +15% on: Teterboro NJ, White Plains NY, Farmingdale NY, Southampton NY, East Hampton NY, Greenwich CT, Manhattan.

S | Routes | FL-NY and S | Pricing-Research | FL-NY

Both states together. Route intent and pricing intent do not depend on which end of the corridor the searcher is sitting at.

Held for month 5: S | Charter | TX and S | Charter | CA

Texas: the whole state, bid adjustment +15% on Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Austin and San Antonio.

California: the whole state, bid adjustment +15% on Los Angeles, Van Nuys, Burbank, Santa Monica, San Francisco, San Jose, Orange County, San Diego and Napa. California is the most expensive and most contested market of the four, which is why it opens last.

S | Brand | US

The entire United States. The brand is defended everywhere.

Ad groups

Campaign 1: S | Brand | US

Ad group Final URL

Minimal budget, low manual cost per click of $2 to $3. The purpose is to stop a competitor buying the company name, and to capture the inexpensive click from someone who already knows the business.

Campaigns 2 and 3: S | Charter | {FL, NY}

Each runs the same 4 ad groups, changing only the city keywords and the landing URL. The same template is what opens Texas and California in month 5:

Ad group Intent Final URL
Charter - Core "private jet charter", "charter a private jet" Campaign landing page
Rental - Hire "private jet rental", "rent a private jet" Campaign landing page
Quote - Book "private jet quote", "book a private jet" Campaign landing page
Cities - {ST} "private jet charter miami", "private jet charter teterboro" The city destination page

A note on the Cities ad group, from the research. City qualified search volume in this vertical is very small: the entire Miami keyword cluster is 6,650 searches a month nationally and its best term is 480. These ad groups are worth running because the traffic is exceptionally high intent and cheap, but they are not where the budget goes. The volume arrives through the generic ad groups, filtered by the campaign's geographic targeting.

The city ad group points at the specific destination page, not the homepage. The site already has them: /destination/miami-private-jet-charters/, /destination/teterboro-private-jet-charters/, /destination/houston-private-jet-charters/, /destination/los-angeles-private-jet-charters/, and many more. Today not a single ad uses any of them.

Campaign 4: S | Routes | FL-NY

Ad group Intent
Routes - Corridor "private jet from new york to miami", "private jet nyc to miami"
Routes - Leisure "private jet to aspen", "private jet to nantucket", "private jet to bahamas"

The most expensive non-curiosity term in the entire account history was private jet from dc to miami at $114.05 across 99 clicks, and it arrived by accident, with no ad group built to serve it. Route intent is the most transactional intent in this vertical: the person already knows where they are going and when. With the live Avinode search on the landing page, it is also the intent we are now best equipped to answer.

Campaign 5: S | Pricing-Research | FL-NY

Ad group Final URL
Pricing - Transactional Campaign landing page, search at the top

This campaign exists for a specific reason. 26.8% of historical spend went to price curiosity, and the instinct is to block that intent entirely. But the campaign landing page now shows real Avinode pricing, which is precisely what that person is looking for. It is the only asset the business has that can convert a price researcher.

So: pure curiosity phrasings are blocked, the transactional phrasings are kept here, the budget is capped at $15 a day so it can never eat into the core campaigns, and it is measured on its own.

The unit economics shortened its leash. At a $350 target cost per lead and a required 17.5% close rate, curiosity traffic cannot pay for itself. private jet cost carries 9,900 searches a month at a low bid of around $3.94, which looks tempting and is precisely the trap this account already fell into. The campaign gets 30 days to produce one booked flight. If it does not, it goes off. It is a capped experiment, not a bet.

Match types

Exact match only at launch, across all 5 campaigns.

Google will still serve close variants of exact match keywords, and that is where the negative list continues to be refined. Phrase and broad match are evaluated in month 2, in separate campaigns with their own budgets, never mixed into the same ad group as exact.

Rule: a keyword lives in exactly one ad group across the account. The current account has 81 keywords repeated across up to 8 ad groups.

Call extension

Number:          +1 800-329-2944
Schedule:        24/7
Call reporting:  ENABLED

Enable call reporting and the Calls from ads conversion with a 60 second minimum duration. Until proper call tracking is in place, this is the only telephone signal available.

Price extension: not loaded yet

Header Services, USD, per flight, qualifier From. Five rows, one per aircraft category.

The prices have to come from you, and they have to be sustainable. A "from $X" that is not honoured is misrepresentation under Google's policies and destroys trust on the call. Until the numbers are firm, this extension stays off.

Location extension

Link the Google Business Profile for 510 Briscoe Blvd Ste 200, Lawrenceville, GA 30046. If one does not exist, create it. It helps ad rank and signals a real company, which carries weight in a vertical full of brokers with no physical presence.

Lead form extension: not at launch

Google's lead form captures a lead without the person ever seeing the landing page, which means no Avinode search, no click identifier in our own database, and no control of the funnel. Reassess in month 3 only if the landing page cost per lead proves high.

Images

Current image assets are named things like Generated image - 2026-08-29 19:28:26_1.199, with several duplicates. Delete them and upload a curated set:

  • Four 1.91:1 and four 1:1 images
  • Cabin interior, exterior on the ramp, private terminal, client boarding
  • No visible tail registration and no operator livery, both because of Avinode's restrictions and because Charter Jets does not operate those aircraft
  • Descriptive filenames, for example cji-cabin-interior-midsize-1x1.jpg

Automatically created assets: switch these off

In a business with real compliance constraints, a Part 135 broker showing estimated prices under Avinode's restrictions, letting Google write ad text unsupervised is an unnecessary risk.
