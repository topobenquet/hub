Keyword Plan

Revised September 3, 2026 with real performance data. Account 645-044-1013 has 86 keywords with click history and 16 that ever produced an inquiry. That evidence reorders this plan. Full detail in account history.

The two keywords that carry the account:

Keyword Spend Clicks Inquiries Cost per inquiry
private jet charter company $19,796 1,806 18 $1,100
private jet charter service $11,154 1,467 13 $858

Together, 46% of all spend and 62% of all conversions. Both carry a provider qualifier, company or service: someone deciding who to hire, not someone researching what a jet costs. These two, and their close variants, get first call on budget and the highest max CPC.

And the cluster to demote. Search terms containing rent or rental took $16,626 and 3,388 clicks. rent a private jet alone took 1,137 clicks across its variants and produced zero inquiries. private jet rental on phrase match spent $1,127 for zero.

We had built Rental - Hire as a peer of Charter - Core. It is not a peer. At launch it runs at a materially lower max CPC, on exact match only, with its own small budget, and it has 30 days to produce an inquiry or it goes off. If you only read one line of this document: charter intent buys, rental intent browses.

Exact match only at launch. Notation: [keyword]. No-duplicates rule: a keyword lives in exactly one ad group within a given geography. The core ad groups (Charter - Core, Rental - Hire, Quote - Book) do repeat across the four state campaigns, and that is correct: those campaigns do not overlap geographically, so they never compete against each other in the same auction. What must not happen, and does happen in the current account, is the same keyword sitting in 8 ad groups inside one campaign.

Suggested max CPC per ad group for the manual bidding phase. These are starting points for the US market, to be recalibrated against real data after 7 days.
