Responsive Search Ads: Full Copy

14 RSAs, 2 per ad group theme, 15 headlines and 4 descriptions each.

Every asset was validated against Google's limits (headline 30, description 90, path 15) using the longest placeholder that will run in production (Los Angeles, 11 characters). Zero assets over the limit.

Token replacement rules

Token What it replaces Maximum length
{CITY} the ad group's city 11 characters. Usar Miami, Ft Lauderdale, Palm Beach, New York, NYC, Teterboro, Dallas, Houston, Austin, Los Angeles, San Diego, Van Nuys, San Jose. Do not use Fort Lauderdale ni San Francisco in headlines, they do not fit
{DEST} the route destination 11 characters. Miami, New York, Las Vegas, Aspen, Nantucket, Los Angeles, Bahamas, Cabo, Napa
{SLUG} the URL path 15 characters, lowercase, hyphenated

If a city does not fit in 11 characters, use its common abbreviation (Ft Lauderdale, SF, OC) or fall back to the generic headline. Never let a headline run over: Google rejects it and the RSA ends up serving with fewer combinations.

Copy constraints observed

  • Charter Jets is a broker, not an operator. No our fleet, no we operate. We use operated by Part 135 certified carriers.
  • ARG/US and Wyvern are presented as ratings held by the operators, not as the company's own credential.
  • The headline Over 100 Years Experience currently running has been removed. On its own it claims the company is over 100 years old, when the description says combined experience. That is misrepresentation, and it is avoidable.
  • Dynamic keyword insertion {KeyWord:...} has been removed from the current ads. With tightly themed ad groups it is no longer needed, and with 396 keywords in one group it was inserting anything at all.
  • The word rental cannot appear in any ad. It is not industry verbiage for a charter broker. The Rental - Hire ad group still buys the rental keywords, which is what you approved, but both of its ads are written entirely in charter language. That costs ad relevance on that ad group, because the keyword term does not appear in the copy. It is an acceptable cost: that cluster spent $16,626 historically with zero conversions on rent a private jet, so it already carries a $12 max CPC and a 30 day kill rule.
  • No multi city in the copy while the Avinode search does not offer it.
  • Pricing is always framed as estimated and before taxes and fees, matching the Avinode widget.

Pinning

Pin nothing at launch. Let Google combine the 15 headlines freely. Pinning reduces the number of possible combinations and lowers ad strength.

The only exception, from week 3 onward and only if the asset report shows a brand headline performing Best: pin Charter Jets Inc to position 3.

What to review at day 21

  1. Asset report per ad group. Replace every headline marked Low.
  2. Ad strength: aim for Good or Excellent across all 20 ad groups.
  3. Compare RSA 1 against RSA 2 on CTR and on inquiry rate, not on conversions, since conversion tracking is only now being rebuilt.
