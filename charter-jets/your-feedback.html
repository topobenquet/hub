Six points came back from you on September 4. Every one of them is well founded. Five were already built into this plan, and the reason is worth explaining, because it changes how much work is left.

Your percentages describe the account we are archiving

You wrote that Google Partners is eating 20% of budget and Display another 10%. Both figures are exact. They come from account 813-172-6321, which is the account this plan already recommends archiving. They do not describe 645-044-1013, the account we are launching in.

Channel 813-172-6321 645-044-1013
Search Partners 20.5% of spend 3.3%: $2,227 and 1,756 clicks, zero conversions
Display / Content 10.4% of spend $0, never ran
Performance Max 18% of spend $0, exists but paused
Phrase and broad match 98.9% of spend Exact match is 65%

This is a useful confirmation rather than a correction. You identified, from the outside, the same three problems the audit found from the inside, and you put them in the same account.

Point by point

1. Make the call or the trip request the primary conversion. Partly done, and this is the only point that opens a real discussion. Every phone link on the landing page now fires a call_click event with its placement, and the sticky call bar now appears on desktop too, where it was previously hidden. What is still missing is a Google forwarding number, which turns a click into a measured call, and the conversion mapping in the account.

The caution: in 645-044-1013 the call extension recorded 7,478 impressions and 8 calls, and desktop converts three times better than mobile. Either the calls were coming through the website and were never measured, which is the likely explanation, or the phone does not carry the weight we assume. Thirty days of call_click data answers it, and waiting costs nothing.

2. Exact match only. Already the plan for all campaigns at launch. The history supports it, though not quite where you would expect: exact match ran at $1,222 per enquiry against $1,848 for phrase, while broad match spent only $1,452 in total. The expensive match type in this account was phrase, at $22,174.

3. Shared charter to negatives. Already there. shared charter, shared flight and shared jet have been on shared negative list 4 since the first build. We have added the pooling variants and the empty leg cluster on top, because empty legs attract the same discount led profile.

4. Remove "rental" from client-facing copy. Done, and it went further than the landing page. The word appeared 17 times on the page, in the title, meta description and social tags, in the H1, in two whole sections and in two FAQ questions. It also appeared across two of the fourteen ads. All of it is now charter language. The keyword list keeps the rental terms, as you specified.

The honest cost: the Rental - Hire ad group now buys rental keywords with charter copy, so its ad relevance will drop. We accept that because the rental cluster spent $16,626 with zero conversions on rent a private jet, so it already carried a $12 max CPC and a 30 day kill rule. If its Quality Score falls below 5, it goes off.

What we gain is larger. The page now matches private jet charter company and private jet charter service, the two keywords that produced 62% of all conversions in the account.

5. Stop Google Search Partners. Done. It was already off in the launch settings. In the account we are using it spent $2,227 across 1,756 clicks and produced nothing, so there is no argument for keeping it.

6. Stop Display. Nothing to stop. Display never ran in 645-044-1013.

7. Performance Max, which you called 50/50. The data settles it as a no. PMax needs roughly 30 to 50 conversions a month to leave its learning period. This account recorded 50 conversions in two years. Feeding PMax that signal is paying Google to explore on your budget. We revisit only after Search sustains 15 or more enquiries a month for two consecutive months.

What this leaves

Nothing on your list blocks the launch. The work it created is small and already done, except for the call measurement, which needs a Google forwarding number and therefore account access. That is on the open items list along with the three things that still matter more than anything else: the GoDaddy access to point lp.charterjetsinc.com at the new page, admin on the account, and how many of the 50 historical enquiries became flights.
